What you get

  • The mow log. Every mow on the record: date, duration, conditions, what you did. Pause for the phone call, pick it back up, finish the job.
  • Your yard, mapped. Trace it once on satellite, split it into zones. Front, back, side. Log exactly what you mowed, and your stats stay honest even when you only hit the front.
  • The streak. Consecutive weeks on the books. See the run, keep the run.
  • Height of cut & the full job. HOC per mow, plus everything you did: mow, trim, edge, blow. The whole session, not just the mowing.
  • Weather, stamped. Conditions captured automatically the moment you save. That 98° July mow counts for what it was.
  • The miles you actually walked. Steps and distance pulled from Apple Health, per mow. Mowing isn't a chore, it's a workout.
